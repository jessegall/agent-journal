# Cedar Lantern welcome desk

A short, approved handover for Saturday volunteers at Cedar Lantern.

## Opening

Arrive at 08:45 for the 09:00 opening. Take the key from the blue lockbox using the code the coordinator gave you in person. Put the sign outside, turn on the desk lamp and open a blank page in the visitor register.

## At the desk

If a visitor asks about a book that is not listed, write its title on a request card for the coordinator. At noon, hand the register and desk key to the next volunteer and mention any books waiting for collection.

## Closing

At 16:00, bring in the sign, switch off the lamp and return the key to the lockbox.
