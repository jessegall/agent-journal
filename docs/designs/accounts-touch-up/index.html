Accounts page: a touch-up

The page as it ships today (AccountRow.vue and AccountsWindow.vue after task 1691), next to the touched-up page, on a phone (390 px) and a wide screen (1600 px, drawn smaller). The settings dialog the button opens stays as it is (option B of doc 71).

1 · A smaller settings buttonThe icon draws at 14 px inside the same 24 px button, so the button is as easy to hit but no longer the loudest thing in the row.
2 · A different iconSliders, drawn in the 16 px box at stroke 1.4 like the rest of the kit. The old gear was a 24 px drawing squeezed in, edge to edge, with thick teeth.
3 · State and expiry move under the name“Signed in · Expires 18 Dec 2026” joins the grey line under the name. The right side holds only buttons, centred in the row.
4 · A centred column on wide screensThe header and the list share a 760 px column, centred; on a wide screen the list is a card with rounded corners. Below 640 px it fills the width, as today.

On a phone

Before: the state column and the cog take about 150 px, so the name truncates early, and “Sign in again” sits stacked under the state. After: the name gets most of the width, the meta line wraps whole pieces, and the button sits beside the settings button.

          The icon

          Each at 40 px to see its drawing, then at the size the row uses. The user found the current gear odd in message 12338 and again now; a gear drawn for 24 px and shrunk keeps its thick teeth.

          Today: gear, 16 px

          Fills 13 of 16 px, heavy outline, the busiest icon on the page.

          Sliders, 14 px Recommended

          Reads as “settings for this one”, light, and matches the kit's line icons.

          Light cog, 14 px

          Still a cog, but with eight short teeth; at 14 px it can look like a sun.

          More (three dots), 14 px

          The quietest, but says “a menu”, and the button opens a dialog.

          Every state of the new row

          The text and the buttons each state shows come from accountLooks.ts and AccountsWindow.vue; only where they sit changes. The settings button is shown in every state except Removing and Removed, as today.

            What could go wrong

            CaseWhat the design does
            A long name on a phoneThe name truncates with its full text in the title, as today; it now has about 100 px more before it does.
            A long meta lineThe pieces (program and billing, Default, state, expiry) wrap as whole pieces to a second line; no piece breaks in the middle.
            A date in another localeThe expiry keeps toLocaleDateString(); a longer date only wraps the meta line and never pushes the buttons.
            Two buttons on a phone (Finish signing in and settings)The right side is about 160 px; the name still keeps about 150 px. Under 360 px the label may shorten to “Finish” — not drawn, worth a check.
            Hover or a dialog open on a wide cardThe hover fill stays inside the card's rounded corners (overflow:hidden); the settings dialog opens centred on the screen as now.
            Recovery notices and the wizardAccountRecovery sits under the list and should share the same 760 px column, or it will run full width under a centred card.
