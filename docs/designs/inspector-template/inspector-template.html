One inspector template

The one layout the journal draws for every row, from what its module declares (doc 1223, revision 3). The task, plan and agent inspectors below are the same template filled differently. A docked card uses the same head. Colours are the viewer's tokens, icons are the viewer's own shapes, and the look follows Agent settings. Frames are live: fold the links, switch tabs, press an action. All data is invented.

The rule on the screen: an action on the panel is an icon only, drawn by the journal in the header. An action on the row is a button with an icon and words, declared by the module, and its words always name the row ("Complete the plan"), so the only Close on screen is the X.

The template, part by part

Who draws what. From the top: the head with the panel actions as icons and the row's type and number, title and description; the module's actions on the row; the tabs; the module's own components in order, then any section another module adds through the slot; and the links section, which folds. The quick comment bar sits at the foot when the module turns comments on.

The four widths

A module names a width and never a number. The viewer keeps the one table of pixels, drawn as the smaller of the width and the screen.

Small, 480A row of text: a memory, a rule, a question
Medium, 640Task, agent, plan, process queue
Large, 960A row with wide parts
Wide, 1130A document with its comment rail, and any row whose comments sit beside it

Docked cards, with the same head

The card's head holds its panel actions as icons only: Keep this card, Open in the inspector, and the X to dismiss it, in the corner. Under the title, the subtext names the row with its number. The module's actions on the row, with icon and words, sit in the foot. A collapsed card keeps Open and the X.
