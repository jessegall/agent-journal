02 · A domain's own page in a window

In version 5 every sidebar page is a window shown on a page of its own: the breadcrumb, then the window, with no agent bars (tests/look/windows/phone-v5.png; windows/PageWindow.vue draws no extra gutter). Version 6 keeps that. A page a domain declares through PageProvider is the same window, with the same component, as in a pane tab or detached. The shell adds nothing to the window's own head. Opening the page on Home is offered from the sidebar row's right-click menu, as in version 5 (SidebarPages.vue pagePlace: Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, Place on Home).

Notes · Collections · Decisions: a page of the outside Notes module, with the sidebar row's menu open

Decisions and Research are records the Notes module lists per request through its children reader. Both point at the one page notes.collection with a different route value. The breadcrumb lists the navigation ancestors after the project; version 5 had project, environment and page, and version 6 has no environment. The page head and New note are the Notes component's own (kit/PageHeader). Layout: kit/ListReaderLayout with the 460 px list, FilterBar and Reader.

Plans · a plan opened in the inspector, with a section from Notes in the slot plans.inspector

On Home and on its page, the Plans window is the same component: version 5's plan cards. A card opens the record in version 5's inspector, which is 640 px at the right edge (resource/InspectorPanel.vue). This settles the difference between cards on Home and rows on the page. Version 5's outside fixture put a section into the plan inspector (acme_plan_notes, slots=("inspector.plan",)). Version 6 does the same: the Plans record page declares the slot plans.inspector, which takes one plan, and Notes declares a section for it. The section frame (34 px head on --bg-2) is new and belongs to the host. The rows belong to Notes.
