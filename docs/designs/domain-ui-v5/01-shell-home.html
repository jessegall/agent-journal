01 · The shell and Home with its windows

Version 5's shell as its source draws it (windows/PaneStage.vue): the 220 px sidebar, the 40 px breadcrumb with the top buttons, the 54 px agent bar, the 30 px information bar, then the pane grid. Every window, sidebar row, dock card and bar part here comes from a domain's declaration. Pink labels name the declaring domain or the version 5 component. Teal labels mark what version 5 did not have. Violet labels mark the parts kept from version 6 because version 6 needs them and version 5 had no equivalent. The bars show only the facts version 6 can read today (decision D3).

Home · three panes and one detached window

Left: Chat (ChatDomain's window), with the dock above the composer: one bordered stack, each card a full-width strip, the card that waits for the person marked by the 2 px amber line, and the stack's fold button at the top because it holds more than one card. Right, top: Plans and Notes as tabs of one pane. Right, bottom: Rules. Floating: Notes · Decisions, detached from a pane. The bar carries a Notes part in the slot web.bar.
