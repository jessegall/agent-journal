04 · Windows: dragging, docking, resizing and saved layouts

Every behaviour drawn here comes from version 5: panes/PaneGrid.vue, ResizeHandle.vue, DragGhost.vue, DropCompass.vue, SplitOffer.vue; kit/FloatWindow.vue, EditorTabs.vue, PresetList.vue, LayoutThumb.vue, MenuPanel.vue, MenuItem.vue; windows/PaneMenu.vue, PaneAddBar.vue; agents/LayoutPicker.vue. The sidebar is hidden with the sidebar button, so the grid has the full width. A window is whatever a domain declared through ProvidesPages; the layout code never knows which domain it belongs to. Pink labels are design notes and are not drawn in the app.

Mid-drag: the Notes tab carried from the right pane onto the left pane

The lifted tab fades to 40 %. The ghost (26 px, --sel, with a shadow) follows the pointer 14/12 px away from it. Over a pane body the drop compass shows five targets, and the zone under the pointer is shaded. Here it is the right half, so dropping splits the Chat pane side by side. Over another tab strip a 2 px accent line shows where the tab lands. Dropping outside every pane detaches the window as a floating one.

Resizing: splitters, the snap to the middle, a detached window's live resize and its minimised bar

A splitter is a 7 px hit area with a 1 px line, invisible until it is hovered (--border-3). While it is dragged, both panes follow live with no easing. Within 14 px of the middle it snaps to 50 % and the line turns accent. A pane never gets narrower than 140 px. A double-click resets the splitter to the middle, and the arrow keys move it 5 %. A detached window moves by its 34 px bar and resizes live by the 14 px grip in its corner. Minimise leaves only the bar, and Dock puts the window back into the layout. A window left outside the screen after the browser shrinks is pulled back until at least 48 px of its bar shows (new; version 5 not checked).

The pane menu, the add bar, presets with a refused name, and the split offer

The pane's "+" opens the add bar. It lists every window the person may see that is not open yet; each can be clicked or dragged. A window the person may not see is absent. A window that cannot open here, for example one that needs a session, is drawn at 45 % with its reason. The pane's "⋮" opens the pane menu: Split right, Split below, Detach, Limit width, the window's declared options, "Move to …", Close and Reset layout. A choice option opens an inner list, and a toggle shows a ✓. The layout button opens the presets. A saved row shows its tools on hover: Save the current layout to this preset, Download, Rename and Delete. "Save layout" names the layout inline, and a name already taken is refused with the picker's notice. Splitting a pane that holds one window asks how first.

Narrow width: stacked panes, compact tabs, the empty pane and the tab menu

Below the grid's narrow width the panes stack, Chat first, each one screen high, snapping as the list scrolls (PaneGrid stacked). In a tight strip the tabs go compact: inactive tabs show only their icon (EditorTabs compact). A right-click on a tab opens version 5's tab menu: Close (Del), Close others, Close to the right, Close all, then Split right, Split below and Detach. A middle click closes a tab. A pane whose last window was closed says "Nothing is open in this pane."
