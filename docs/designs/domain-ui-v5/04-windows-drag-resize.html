04 · Windows: dragging, docking, resizing and saved layouts

Every behaviour here is version 5's (panes/PaneGrid.vue, ResizeHandle.vue, DragGhost.vue, DropCompass.vue, SplitOffer.vue, kit/FloatWindow.vue, windows/PaneMenu.vue, PaneAddBar.vue, agents/LayoutPicker.vue). The sidebar is hidden with the top bar's sidebar button to give the grid the full width. A window is whatever a domain declared through ProvidesPages; the layout code never knows which domain it is.

Mid-drag: the Notes tab carried from the right pane onto the left pane

The lifted tab fades to 40 %. The ghost (26 px, --sel, shadow) follows the pointer 14/12 px off. Over a pane body the drop compass shows five targets; the zone under the pointer is shaded (here: right half, so dropping splits the Chat pane side by side). Over another tab strip a 2 px accent line shows where the tab lands (bottom pane). Dropping outside every pane detaches the window into a floating one.

Resizing: splitters, snap to the middle, a detached window's grip and the minimised bar

A splitter is a 7 px hit area with a 1 px line, invisible until hovered (--border-3); dragging moves both panes live with no easing; within 14 px of the middle it snaps to 50 % and the line turns accent; a pane never gets below 140 px; double-click resets to the middle; arrow keys move it 5 %. A detached window moves by its 34 px bar and resizes by the 14 px grip in its corner; minimise leaves only the bar; Dock puts it back into the layout.

Pane menu, add-a-window bar, presets and the split offer

The "+" of a pane opens the add bar with every window not yet open (version 5 PaneAddBar), each one drag-able or click-able. The "⋮" opens the pane menu: split, detach, the options the window declared, move to another pane, close. The layout button of the information bar opens the presets (thumbnails) with Save layout and Import. Splitting a pane with one window first asks how (SplitOffer).
