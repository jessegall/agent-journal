03 · The sidebar with nested navigation

Version 5's sidebar (shell/SidebarPages.vue): 220 px, a fold-head per group (11 px, 600, uppercase), rows 6×8 padding, 7 px radius, the count at the right, the foot line, and the 52 px mini form. New in version 6: a row may hold child rows to any depth. The link opens the page; the separate chevron opens or closes the children without navigating.

Expanded, collapsed, mini and phone

A: every level open, the current page five levels deep; indentation stops after level 3 and the branch line turns dashed, a long label ends in an ellipsis and its full path shows in the tooltip. B: the same tree with Notes closed, the dot telling that the current page is inside, and the Agents group folded. C: mini sidebar, children in a flyout on hover or focus. D: phone width, the drawer with 44 px targets over the version 5 phone bar.
