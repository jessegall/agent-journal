Workflows page, revised: steps you can read without opening them

A refinement of the Workflows page as it is built today (Event, Run order, the chart and its side panel), not a new layout. Two things were slow: a step on the chart cut its words short, so you opened the side panel to learn what a check asks or what an action says; and the side panel then showed everything about the step at once.

What changes: a step shows its question or its message in full, wrapped at whole words; a check that reads a setting says the setting’s number and name; a message shows three lines and says how many words follow. The side panel leads with the step’s one thing and folds the rest. The same holds in Steps at a phone’s width. Everything else on the page stays where it is: the lists, the bar, the buttons, the ports, the lit run, the dialogs.

Colours, type, radii and spacing are the viewer’s own, from tokens.css and the chart’s own styles. The example is the shipped workflow at Pre tool use, Hold a write while messages stay unread after 5 reminders.

Words wrap at whole words, never cut mid-wordA question cut in half cannot be read. The question shows in full on as many lines as it needs.

A message shows three lines, then how many words followEnough to know what the agent is told; the count says there is more. The reminder’s name in brackets moves to the side panel as Sent as the reminder; values show as marked words, unread count, not the code in double braces.

What the page needs that it does not have: the setting’s title, value and unit beside each check that names one, so the card can say 5 is the setting Hold the write after; and, for the next plan, the agent’s count now and the step where its last walk stopped.
