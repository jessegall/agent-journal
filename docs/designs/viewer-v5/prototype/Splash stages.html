Opening the journal: the splash

First request
Demo hold

The splash waits until the tab is in view, then holds a blank screen for half a second. The ring appears with the dot in its centre and stays; the dot pulses, a double beat every second, for about 2 s. Then the gap opens and the ring spins: at least two full turns, longer while the first request is still loading. Only then does the end play, always whole, and Home shows. Demo hold keeps the spin going for at least 4 s, so you can feel what loading looks like; it is on by default in the prototype.
