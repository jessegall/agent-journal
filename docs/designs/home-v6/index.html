Grows
Window

One Home with two ends. On day one it is a plain chat anyone understands; grown by hand, it becomes something like an IDE. To dock a window into the pane grid (version 6's paneLayout.ts), drag it past an edge of Home, onto a pane's compass, or onto a tab strip. Panes share edges you can drag, and windows you take out float. Press ⌘K for every command. The chat stays clean: what agents do goes to the Activity panel, and only what you choose reaches the chat. Documents open in a reader, take comments, and can be dragged onto an agent. The Tasks and Plans pages show the task graph. Every page in the sidebar works, all in the app's own tokens and shell.

Variants B (tiled columns), C (chat and a drawer) and D (board of cards) are retired. They are described in doc 101, round 1, and kept in this file's git history.
