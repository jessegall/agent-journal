Grows
Window

One Home with two ends. On day one it is a plain chat anyone understands at first sight; step by step it grows, if the user wants, into something like an IDE: a Project tree, an editor with tabs, an Activity panel, a status bar with shortcuts and notifications. Every step is the same mechanism. Press Space (or ⌘K) to add a window. Drag it to a side, corner or the top edge to snap it flush, or drag it away to float it again. Double-click a bar to fill or restore it, and right-click anything for its menu. Fold the sidebar (⌘B) or hide the agent bar (⌘⇧B) for room, and press ? for every shortcut. The chat is always one of two things: the plain centred conversation with no frame, or a proper window with its own bar. Tabs are flat, with a line under the active one. In step 6, open a file from Project, select lines and press Comment to talk to any agent about that code. Rounds 2 and 3 build on A, the floating desk; B, C and D stay to compare.
