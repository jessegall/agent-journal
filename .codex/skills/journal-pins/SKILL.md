# Pins

The environment's pins said again to the agent at every tenth of the context.

A pin is a fact a later reader would get wrong without; it is handed back as the window fills.

It listens to: agent.updated. It speaks every 10 percent. On by default; the viewer's Settings switches it per environment, and `triggers.pins` in the environment's settings tunes it.
