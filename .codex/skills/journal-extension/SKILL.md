# The chat that follows you home

The Chrome shell carries the journal chat across tabs and points at the page beneath it.

Always available from Settings as a zip, with its window, picker and browser-driving bridge kept in the package.

It listens to: . It speaks on the events it listens to. On by default; the viewer's Settings switches it per environment, and `triggers.extension` in the environment's settings tunes it.
