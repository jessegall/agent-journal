# Rules

The rules said again at every tenth of the context, and the injected ones kept in CLAUDE.md.

A rule binds every environment; inject puts it in CLAUDE.md and every change rewrites that block.

It listens to: agent.updated, rule. It speaks every 10 percent. On by default; the viewer's Settings switches it per environment, and `triggers.rules` in the environment's settings tunes it.
