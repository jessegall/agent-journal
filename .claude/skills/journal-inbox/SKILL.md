# The inbox

Unread messages are named after a tool use, again and again, and past a patience they hold the agent's writes.

Said at the first tool use after a message arrives and every third after; five times ignored, the hold. triggers.inbox sets the cadence, inbox.patience the count.

It listens to: agent.updated, message.created. It speaks every 3 uses. On by default; the viewer's Settings switches it per environment, and `triggers.inbox` in the environment's settings tunes it.
