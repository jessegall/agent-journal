# Suggestions

A suggestion is a change the agent thinks would help but nobody asked for. Something it noticed along the way, like a refactor, a fix or a better approach.

## Who decides

You do. The agent does not act on a suggestion until you say so, and it keeps working on what you asked for in the meantime.

## What you can do

- **Accept** it, and it becomes work for the agent.
- **Adjust** it: accept it with changes of your own.
- **Decline** it, with a reason if you like.

Declined and withdrawn suggestions are hidden until you switch them on.
